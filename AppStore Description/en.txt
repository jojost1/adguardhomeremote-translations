AdGuard Home Remote makes managing your AdGuard Home server quick and easy from iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch, and Apple TV. Toggle blocking, view stats, and manage your clients, filters, and DNS rules. All from a clean, native app.

*Control Blocking*
You can quickly enable or disable filtering & safe browsing/search. You can even manage specific settings for your clients!

*Statistics*
View graphs of your queries as well as top clients and domains. Search through your queries in the Query Log with detailed request and response info. Share your stats as a sleek graphic in just a tap.

*Manage Settings*
Add or edit your clients, add some new filters or maybe disable them for a while, update your filters, edit blocked services, manage DHCP and DNS settings, create new DNS rules, or edit your Query Log settings.

*Multiple Instances, Synced*
Running more than one AdGuard Home server? Switch between them in a tap, sync settings between instances, and let Away Connection automatically pick the right one based on your Wi-Fi (requires Pro).

*System Integration*
Live Activity, Home Screen & Lock Screen widgets, Control Center controls, Siri Shortcuts, and a Share Extension to quickly block or unblock domains straight from your browser. AdGuard Home Remote is everywhere you need it.

*iPhone, iPad, Mac, Watch & Apple TV*
Specifically crafted to be the best app on every platform.
